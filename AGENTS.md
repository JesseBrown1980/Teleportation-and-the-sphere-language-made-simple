# Sphere Language Repository Discipline

- The verbatim operator intake appears exactly once, in
  `books/JESSE-TO-RAYSSA-SPHERE-LANGUAGE-SOURCE.md`. Derived artifacts bind its
  SHA-256 and must not copy the passage.
- Preserve `V = VELOCITY`; `Vector = V + e + c + t + o + r`. A component is not
  the composed word.
- Preserve both operator readings of `Live`: `LOOK_TO_E_AND_BE` and
  `LOOK_FROM_LIFE_AND_BE`. They are simultaneously addressable spherical readings;
  neither replaces the other.
- Preserve `NO = INFINITE_AND_END`, `END = E + N + D`, `D = DIE_STOP` as operator
  grammar, `STOP = BINARY`, `F = FROM_ACTION`, and `L = LIFE`. The Hebrew association
  remains `OPERATOR_CANON` with historical etymology `UNVERIFIED`.
- Keep glyph, glyph-function, letter, word, CALLING, CALMING, FLOWe traversal,
  tuple-command, matrix view, and executable authority as separate ledgers.
- `CALLING_JOIN` retains both endpoints and has unresolved direction until the
  operator or owning executable contract binds it. `FLOWe` is an ordered traversal.
- Use signed integer coordinates for matrix views. A 2-D projection is a view of
  the 3-D matrix, not the identity of the matrix.
- Tag the new meanings `OPERATOR_CANON`. Tag byte/hash/test results `MEASURED`.
  Tag a physical, historical-linguistic, Hebrew-etymological, or runtime inference
  `UNVERIFIED` until its owning evidence surface measures it.
- Active receipts use HBP/HBI tuple text with `json=0`. Keep
  `{HBI,HBP,SHA,SH,HASH}` distinct and in that active traversal order; `SH` remains
  operator-canon unresolved.
- Keep credentials, private keys, cookies, private archaeology, and health data out
  of the repository. Public decoders require zero hidden account secrets.
- Preserve candidates in reviewable Git history and use positive fail-closed gates.
